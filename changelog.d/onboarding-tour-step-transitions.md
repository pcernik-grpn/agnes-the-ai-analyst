### Fixed
- **"Next" in the guided tour no longer looks like a dead button when the next step lives on another page.** Advancing from the /chat steps to the /library ones is a full-page navigation, and the browser keeps painting the outgoing card until the destination is ready — so the press was answered by nothing at all, for as long as /library took to load (reported as "won't let me click next", with the reader waiting 5-10s before it moved). The card now goes into a pending state the instant it is pressed: actions disabled, `aria-busy`, and an indeterminate bar along its top edge. The hop is also single-shot, so mashing "Next" through the wait can no longer fire it twice and skip the two steps in between.
- **The "Put it in your stack" step no longer rings a control that is off screen.** Its second highlight (the "In stack only" filter) was applied before the page scrolled to the step's own anchor, which on a populated Library left the ring roughly 490px above the fold — painted, real, and impossible to find. Extra highlights are now applied after the anchor scroll lands, and only to elements actually in the viewport.
- **A guided-tour card no longer covers the column of controls it is describing.** Horizontal placement aligned the card's left edge to its anchor's and clamped it back inside the viewport, which for an anchor near the right edge parked it directly under that anchor's own column. In the Library that hid the per-row controls the step was pointing at — "Put it in your stack" covered 8 of 9 "Add" buttons while telling the reader to click Add on a row, and "Share what turns out to be useful" covered the sharing badges while telling them every row shows who can see it. The card is now placed beside such an anchor, to its left, so the column stays readable; anchors with room to their right keep the previous placement.
- **The element a guided-tour step highlights now actually reads as highlighted.** Two separate cascade failures, both because `.tour-spotlight` is a single class whose declarations were not `!important`. Its opaque fill lost to any two-class page rule, so an anchor with no background of its own showed the dimmed page straight through and read as disabled inside its own bright ring (measured 67 RGB darker than true colour on the Library's "Add to my agents" and sharing-badge steps, 72 on the Connect coach-mark) — the fill is now written inline, and only where the computed background is fully transparent, so anchors that have one (the primary blue buttons on Library and Agents) keep it untouched. Separately, the chat composer's own focus ring replaced the spotlight's box-shadow outright, and /chat autofocuses the composer, so the first step of the tour opened with no ring at all until the reader happened to click elsewhere; the spotlight ring now outranks whatever the target draws for itself.
