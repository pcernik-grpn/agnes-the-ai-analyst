### Added
- **Operator docs now say who a Google OAuth client actually admits, and how to hand the Google door to a customer.** [`docs/auth-google-oauth.md`](docs/auth-google-oauth.md) gained an *Audience* section: the consent screen's Internal/External choice gates sign-in BEFORE `auth.allowed_domain` does, Internal is scoped to the Workspace account that owns the Cloud project (so several email domains pass only as secondary domains of that one account), External needs publishing but no verification for the `openid email profile` scopes, and an instance carries exactly one Google client. New [`docs/auth-google-oauth-customer-setup.md`](docs/auth-google-oauth-customer-setup.md) is the checklist for the shape where the customer registers the client in their own project and keeps it Internal — what to ask their Google admin for, what comes back, why the operator's own staff then need the password door, and the three refusals to walk when verifying.

### Fixed
- **`docs/auth-google-oauth.md` no longer claims `SERVER_URL` pins the Google callback URL.** `google_login()` builds `redirect_uri` from `request.url_for("google_callback")`, so a callback arriving on the wrong host is a proxy-header problem; `SERVER_URL` / `AGNES_BASE_URL` govern other external links and the `Secure` cookie flag. The failure table now also carries `org_internal` and `admin_policy_enforced`.
