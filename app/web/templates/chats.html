{# ── Filter ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── ONE button for every way of narrowing the list, the Library's pattern exactly: a click opens a grouped popover, and each applied CATEGORY shows as a removable chip on the row above so the resting bar stays one line. The four views (All · Pinned · Shared · Archived) live at the TOP of that popover rather than as a segmented control on the bar. As tabs they were a second, louder filter sitting beside the Filter button — two controls doing one job, and the wider one spent its width on three counts a caller reads once. In here they are what they are: the first thing you choose when you narrow the list. They stay SINGLE-select (a chat is shown in one view at a time), which is why they remain the engine's `segments` control rather than becoming checkbox facets: `data-own` is the value it reads, the four are mutually exclusive, and "no selection" is not a state they have — unlike a facet, where it means "don't filter" and would let archived conversations back into the default view. Counts are the UNFILTERED tally per view — the same convention the categories below use — and chats_page.js recomputes them after any action, so archiving four chats moves four out of All and into Archived without a reload. #}
