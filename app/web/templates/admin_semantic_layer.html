{% elif active_tab == 'health' %}

Health

Coverage answers what exists; this answers whether what exists is broken, stale, or internally inconsistent — sync failures, models whose source was deleted or renamed away from under them, documents that failed validation, and three static checks over the documents themselves. A finding already muted is still listed here, marked as silenced, never dropped — silently omitting it would be exactly the anonymous disappearance muting exists to prevent.
