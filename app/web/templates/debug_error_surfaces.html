All three surfaces share one component. Tone is three quiet signals — a light ground, a stripe down the left edge, and a 16px glyph — while the sentence itself stays in body ink. Coloured text on a coloured ground was most of what made these read as alarms, and all of what cost them legibility.

In the transcript

What a reader sees when a turn ends early. The tone chip is chatErrorTone()'s verdict: only a genuine failure gets the red treatment — a rate limit, a restart or a spent budget resolves by waiting or by something the reader can do, so those read amber.
